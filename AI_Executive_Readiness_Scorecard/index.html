How ready are you and your organization’s leaders to lead with AI?

AI changes the decisions leaders make and the work they ask people to do. Assess your organization’s readiness to lead AI-related change by measuring it across four dimensions. Answer 12 questions for both yourself and your organization’s leadership team, then compare the two profiles. Allow about 15 minutes.
